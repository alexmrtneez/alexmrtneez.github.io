About

I am a Master's student in Data Science & Computer Engineering at the University of Granada, with a Double Degree in Computer Science & Mathematics. Within Computer Science I specialised in both Artificial Intelligence and Software Engineering, so I enjoy building systems that are both well engineered and data-driven.

In my bachelor's thesis I carried out a rigorous analysis of spatial transcriptomics data, supported by an interactive R Shiny web application I developed to simulate, explore and analyse this kind of data.

During a six-month research grant with the MYDASS group (CITIC-UGR), I designed the data pipeline for a wearable-sensor stress study.

I'm keen to keep growing as a software engineer and data scientist, and always happy to hear about internships or junior roles where I can contribute.
